PUBLIC TESTING

Report a problem

Answer what you can; every question is optional. Your answers become one short paragraph to email, with the log attached. Nothing is sent anywhere until you send the email yourself.

1Your computer

Hot Mic fills this in when the page is opened from its menu. Settings › About shows the version.

2What went wrong?

Choose all that apply.

3How often?
4Where were you typing, and with which microphone?
5What you said, and what appeared

Only if you're happy to share it. A rough idea is fine.

6Anything else

In your own words: what you were doing, what you expected, anything that seemed to cause it.

Your report

This updates as you answer.

Open in email

Then attach the log

The log has times and settings, never what you said. It helps find the cause quickly.

  • Windows: right-click the Hot Mic icon by the clock › Report a problem. A folder opens with hotmic.log selected: drag it into the email.
  • Mac: click the Hot Mic icon in the menu bar › Report a problem. Finder shows hotmic.log: drag it into the email.
