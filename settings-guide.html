Settings Guide

For Hot Mic 0.5.2 and later on Windows

Hot Mic works well without changing anything. This guide explains each setting, and suggests the settings that suit common computers and needs. To open Settings, right-click the microphone icon near the clock (it may be behind the ^ arrow) and choose Settings.

Recommended settings

Find the description closest to the computer and the person using it. Anything not mentioned can stay as it is.

GAMING PC

A desktop with a graphics card

For example, a PC used for games or a flight simulator.

  • Performance: Automatic. Hot Mic uses the graphics card normally, and steps down while a game is running so the game comes first.
  • Use the graphics card for smart cleanup: on.
  • Whisper for muffled mics: on.
  • Cleanup: Smart, with Skip the AI when there's nothing to fix on.
LAPTOP

No strong graphics card

Most laptops, and older or office computers.

  • Performance: Automatic, or Lightest if the laptop gets hot or slow.
  • Cleanup: Smart, with Skip the AI when there's nothing to fix on. This saves the most time on a laptop.
  • Basic cleanup for very long dictations: on, if long dictations feel slow.
  • Whisper for muffled mics: it needs a graphics card, so it stays inactive here.
LOW VISION

Everything without reading small text

Hot Mic is designed for this first.

  • Play sounds: on (the default). Each step has its own sound.
  • Indicator style: Bold, size Extra large, colours White on black or Yellow on black.
  • Position: Middle of the screen, if the bottom is hard to see.
  • Read the text aloud after typing it: on, to hear what was typed.
HEADSETS

Bluetooth headsets and muffled mics

Bluetooth headsets send lower-quality sound while their microphone is in use.

  • Microphone: choose the headset by name (Advanced), rather than Windows default.
  • Whisper for muffled mics: on (needs a graphics card). It is a second, stronger listener for muffled sound.
  • My words: add names that are often misheard.
LONG DICTATIONS

Paragraphs, essays and long messages

Hot Mic transcribes and tidies long dictations while the person is still talking, so little is left when they stop.

  • Pause briefly between sentences: Hot Mic works on each finished sentence during the pause.
  • Stop listening automatically after: raise it from 300 seconds if dictations are longer than five minutes.
  • Basic cleanup for very long dictations: on, for the fastest results.
CHAT APPS

Discord, Messages and similar

For sending messages hands-free.

  • Voice command “press enter”: on (the default). Saying “press enter” at the very end sends the message.
  • Typing the text in: Paste (the default). Choose Type letter by letter only for an app that blocks pasting.

Mac test version: it has no Settings window yet. It uses the defaults: right ⌥ Option to start and stop, the Wood sounds, and the Quiet indicator.

If something isn't right

  • The text was left on the clipboard (a message says to press Ctrl+V): the app didn't accept the paste in time, or another window was in front. Pressing Ctrl+V pastes it. If it happens often, the log (Advanced › Log folder) records the reason.
  • Words are misheard: check the right microphone is chosen, add names to My words, and for a Bluetooth headset turn on Whisper.
  • It feels slow: leave Skip the AI when there's nothing to fix on. On a laptop, also try Basic cleanup for very long dictations, or Basic cleanup for everything.
  • A game or simulator stutters: keep Performance on Automatic or choose Lightest.
