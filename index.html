How it works

From speech to text in three steps.

  1. 1

    Press F9

    Click in any text box, in any app. A soft sound tells you Hot Mic is listening.

  2. 2

    Speak naturally

    “Um”s, repeats and corrections such as “Thursday, no, Friday” are tidied away. Say “new line” or “scratch that” to control it.

  3. 3

    Press F9 again

    A second sound, and the punctuated text appears in a second or two.

Private by design

Speech recognition runs entirely on your computer. Audio is never uploaded. No account, no subscription, no tracking. Privacy Policy

Designed for low vision

A distinct sound for each step, a large high-contrast indicator, read-back, and settings that work with the keyboard and screen readers.

Writes like you would

“At eight thirty” becomes “at 8:30”. Names and terms you add to My words are spelled your way every time.

Light on your computer

Works with wired and Bluetooth headsets, and steps back automatically while games or flight simulators are running.

Keeps itself up to date

When a new version is ready, Hot Mic shows a notice. Click it to update in about 15 seconds, or press Update now on the Home page of Settings. Otherwise it updates by itself when the computer is left alone, never while you’re dictating or playing.

Installing

Ready in four steps.

The same four steps on Windows and on a Mac. Steps 1 to 3 happen once; after that, it’s just step 4. No administrator rights are needed.

Windows 10 and 11

  1. 1

    Download Hot Mic

    Select Download for Windows, then open HotMic-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.

  2. 2

    Allow it to open

    Windows may say it protected your PC. Select More info, then Run anyway.

    This appears because Hot Mic is new and not yet code-signed. It doesn’t mean anything is wrong.

    Windows protected your PC
    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting.
    More info
    Run anyway Don’t run
  3. 3

    Finish in the Welcome window

    Select Download. The free AI models (about 1.3 GB) download once.

  4. 4

    Start dictating

    Click in any app, press F9, speak, and press F9 again.

Mac test version · Apple silicon (M1 or later)

  1. 1

    Download Hot Mic

    Select Download for Mac, open HotMic-Mac.dmg from your Downloads folder, and drag Hot Mic onto Applications.

  2. 2

    Allow it to open

    Open Hot Mic from Applications. macOS may say it couldn’t verify Hot Mic. Select Done, then:

    1. Open Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security.
    2. Under Security, select Open Anyway next to “Hot Mic” was blocked.
    3. Enter your Mac password, then select Open Anyway again.

    This appears because Hot Mic is new and not yet registered with Apple. It doesn’t mean anything is wrong. The Open Anyway button shows for about an hour after opening Hot Mic.

    Security
    “Hot Mic” was blocked to protect your Mac. Open Anyway
  3. 3

    Finish in the Welcome window

    Allow the Microphone, turn on Hot Mic under Accessibility (so it can notice its key and type), then select Download. The free AI models (about 1.3 GB) download once.

  4. 4

    Start dictating

    Click in any app, press right ⌥ Option, speak, and press it again.

    The test version doesn’t update itself yet. Release notes and known limits.

System requirements
  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A graphics card is optional and makes it faster.
  • Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or later) with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple menu › About This Mac shows the chip.
  • Both: a microphone (headset, webcam or built-in) and about 2 GB of free disk space for the AI models.
