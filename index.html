HOW IT WORKS

From speech to text in three steps.

1

Press F9

Place the cursor in any text field, in any application. A soft tone confirms that Hot Mic is listening.

2

Speak naturally

Filler words, repetitions and self-corrections such as “Thursday, no, Friday” are removed automatically. Spoken commands such as “new line” and “scratch that” are also supported.

3

Press F9 again

A second tone confirms the recording has ended, and the punctuated text appears within a second or two.

Private by design

Speech recognition runs entirely on the computer. Audio is never uploaded, and Hot Mic requires no account or subscription and contains no tracking. Privacy Policy

Designed for low vision

Each step has a distinct sound cue, a large high-contrast indicator is available, and every setting works with the keyboard and screen readers.

Natural formatting

Spoken numbers are written as people write them: “at eight thirty” becomes “at 8:30”. Names and terms added to My words are always spelled correctly.

Light on system resources

Works with standard and Bluetooth headsets, and automatically reduces its resource use while games or flight simulators are running.

INSTALLING

Ready in four steps.

The same four steps on Windows and on a Mac. Steps 1 to 3 are only needed once; after that, it’s just step 4. No administrator rights are required.

Windows 10 and 11

1

Download Hot Mic

Select Download for Windows, then open HotMic-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.

2

Allow it to open

Windows may say it protected your PC. Select More info, then Run anyway.

This message appears because Hot Mic is new and not yet code-signed. It doesn’t mean anything is wrong.

Windows protected your PC
Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting.
More info
Run anyway Don’t run
3

Finish in the Welcome window

Select Download. The free AI models (about 1.3 GB) download once.

4

Start dictating

Place the cursor in any app, press F9, speak, and press F9 again.

Hot Mic keeps itself up to date.

Mac test version · Apple silicon (M1 or later)

1

Download Hot Mic

Select Download for Mac, then open HotMic-Mac.dmg from your Downloads folder and drag Hot Mic onto Applications.

2

Allow it to open

Open Hot Mic from Applications. macOS may say it couldn’t verify Hot Mic. Select Done, then:

  1. Open Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  2. Under Security, select Open Anyway next to “Hot Mic” was blocked.
  3. Enter your Mac password, then select Open Anyway again.

This message appears because Hot Mic is new and not yet registered with Apple. It doesn’t mean anything is wrong. The Open Anyway button shows for about an hour after opening Hot Mic.

Security
“Hot Mic” was blocked to protect your Mac. Open Anyway
3

Finish in the Welcome window

Allow the Microphone, turn on Hot Mic under Accessibility (so it can notice its key and type), then select Download. The free AI models (about 1.3 GB) download once.

4

Start dictating

Place the cursor in any app, press right ⌥ Option, speak, and press it again.

The test version doesn’t update itself yet. Release notes and known limits.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A graphics card is optional and makes it faster.
  • Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or later) with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple menu › About This Mac shows the chip.
  • Both: a microphone (headset, webcam or built-in) and about 2 GB of free disk space for the AI models.
