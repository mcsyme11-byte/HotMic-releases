HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, then it just types.

1

Press F9

In any app, wherever your cursor is. One soft ring tells you Hot Mic is listening.

2

Talk normally

Ums, repeats and “Thursday, no, Friday” are tidied for you. Say “new line” or “scratch that” when you need to.

3

Press F9 again

Two rings, and your words appear, punctuated, in a second or two.

Private by design

The speech AI runs on your own PC. Your voice is never uploaded, and there is no account, subscription or tracking.

Made for low vision

Every step has its own sound, a large high-contrast indicator is one switch away, and everything works from the keyboard and screen readers.

Writes like you would

“At eight thirty” becomes “at 8:30”. Names you add to My words are spelled your way, every time.

Gets out of the way

Copes with ordinary and Bluetooth headsets, and steps aside while you play a game or fly a simulator.

INSTALLING

A few clicks, no password needed.

  1. 1 Open HotMic-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. 2 If Windows shows a blue warning, click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because Hot Mic is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate, not because anything is wrong.
  3. 3 Click Download in the Welcome window. The free AI models come down once (about 1.3 GB), then Hot Mic is ready and keeps itself up to date.
WHAT YOU NEED
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • A microphone: a headset, webcam or laptop mic is fine
  • About 2 GB of free space for the AI models
  • A graphics card is optional; it makes Hot Mic quicker
