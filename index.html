HOW IT WORKS

From speech to text in three steps.

1

Press F9

Place the cursor in any text field, in any application. A soft tone confirms that Hot Mic is listening.

2

Speak naturally

Filler words, repetitions and self-corrections such as “Thursday, no, Friday” are removed automatically. Spoken commands such as “new line” and “scratch that” are also supported.

3

Press F9 again

A second tone confirms the recording has ended, and the punctuated text appears within a second or two.

Private by design

Speech recognition runs entirely on the computer. Audio is never uploaded, and Hot Mic requires no account or subscription and contains no tracking. Privacy Policy

Designed for low vision

Each step has a distinct sound cue, a large high-contrast indicator is available, and every setting works with the keyboard and screen readers.

Natural formatting

Spoken numbers are written as people write them: “at eight thirty” becomes “at 8:30”. Names and terms added to My words are always spelled correctly.

Light on system resources

Works with standard and Bluetooth headsets, and automatically reduces its resource use while games or flight simulators are running.

INSTALLING

Simple installation, with no administrator rights required.

  1. 1 Open HotMic-Setup.exe from the Downloads folder.
  2. 2 If Windows displays a blue warning, select More info, then Run anyway. The warning appears because Hot Mic is new and is not yet signed with a commercial code-signing certificate; it does not indicate a problem with the software.
  3. 3 Select Download in the Welcome window. The free AI models (about 1.3 GB) are downloaded once. Hot Mic is then ready to use and keeps itself up to date.
INSTALLING ON A MAC · TEST VERSION

Four steps, the first time only.

Hot Mic is free and is not yet registered with Apple, so macOS asks for confirmation the first time it is opened. After that, it opens normally.

1

Copy Hot Mic to Applications

Open the downloaded HotMic-Mac.dmg and drag Hot Mic onto the Applications folder.

2

Open it once, then select Done

Open Hot Mic from Applications. macOS shows a message that it could not verify the app. Select Done. This is expected for apps that are not registered with Apple.

“Hot Mic” Not Opened
Apple could not verify “Hot Mic” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
Done Move to Trash
3

Allow it in System Settings

  1. Open the Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to Security. Next to “Hot Mic” was blocked, select Open Anyway. The button appears for about an hour after step 2.
  3. Enter your Mac password, then select Open Anyway once more.
Security
“Hot Mic” was blocked to protect your Mac. Open Anyway
4

Give Hot Mic its two permissions

Hot Mic's Welcome window guides you through both, one at a time:

  • Microphone: select Allow when macOS asks.
  • Accessibility: lets Hot Mic notice its key and type text. Turn on the switch next to Hot Mic in Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

The AI models then download once (about 1.3 GB). Press right ⌥ Option in any app to start dictating.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • Mac (test version): Apple silicon (M1 or later) with macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • A microphone: a headset, webcam or built-in laptop microphone
  • About 2 GB of free disk space for the AI models
  • On Windows, a dedicated graphics card is optional and improves speed
